# ShareFresh
Food Inventory And Giveaway List creator to organize the pantry. It aims to reduce food waste by calculating the date which food can be given away for free based on fda recommendations assuming food was handled with proper hygiene protocols. 

# Features
- Calculate suggested giveaway dates.
- Preloaded food storage recommendations.
- Add custom products and storage durations.
- Print or download calculated results.

# Data Sources

FreshDate uses food storage reference information from publicly available food safety resources.

Primary references include:

- U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) FoodKeeper.
- USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS).
- U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA).
- FoodSafety.gov.

Storage estimates are used for planning purposes and should be verified against applicable food safety guidance, product labeling, and actual storage conditions.

References are provided for informational purposes and do not constitute an endorsement of FreshDate by these organizations.
